Diagram Presentation

Manual checks for #9. This page's own CSS hides every svg, turns buttons pink and puts red dotted borders on every div. None of that should show up on the Diagrams or their controls.

  • A wide diagram fits the column. The small one stays at its natural size.
  • +, − and reset appear next to the toggle once a Diagram shows. − and reset are disabled when fitted.
  • Ctrl+wheel over a Diagram zooms around the cursor. A plain wheel scrolls the page.
  • When zoomed in, dragging pans. The text below the Diagram never moves while you zoom or pan.
  • Switch Firefox to dark (Settings → General → Website appearance → Dark), then flip a Diagram off and on: it's drawn in Mermaid's dark theme on a dark frame.
  • Tab reaches every control, and Enter or Space works.
